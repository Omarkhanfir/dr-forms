The stationary target is preserved alongside one chasing enemy. No waves or assessment features were added.

Inspector settings: speed 2.5 units/second, contact damage 20, contact cooldown 1 second, contact distance 1.1 units; enemy maximum health is 100. Direct pursuit stops near the player and uses arena/floor collisions; it does not include obstacle pathfinding.
- Player maximum health (100) is Inspector-configurable. A safe-area HUD health bar/numeric label updates on damage; zero health blocks gameplay, cancels projectiles, and shows a blocking DEFEATED overlay with RESTART.
- Restart reloads GamePrototype, restoring player/enemy/practice-target health and spawns, camera, combat/contact counters and cooldowns, and clearing all projectiles, effects and old control owners. Held buttons/touches must reach a neutral frame; then fresh input is accepted. A stationary desktop cursor cannot resume an old destination, but a new click can fire without moving the cursor. An Editor-only scene-path reload supports the prototype without changing the default build scene list.
- The WebGL canvas bridge cancels input on blur/hidden tabs/pointer cancellation. Desktop pointer exit and a joystick thumb dragged outside the game stop movement. Stale destinations/touches do not resume after cancellation.
